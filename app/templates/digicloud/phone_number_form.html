Number Assignment Guide

REMOVE FROM DOMAIN

Removes the number from its current DigiCloud domain and places it in NOP Phone Numbers as Available for future assignment. The number is not removed from your carrier account.

MOVE / REASSIGN

Assign the number to another DigiCloud domain or change its treatment. If no treatment is selected, the number remains in the selected DigiCloud domain and is assigned the Available Number treatment.

REMOVE FROM CARRIER

Removing a number from your carrier account is performed through LNP Management. This page manages DigiCloud phone number assignments only.

Important Notes

  • Removing a number from a DigiCloud domain removes it from that domain and places it in NOP Phone Numbers as Available for future assignment.
  • This action does not remove the number from your carrier account.
  • To permanently remove a number from your carrier account, use LNP Management.
